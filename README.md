I am **Co-founder and CTO at [ADB Technologies](https://adbtech.ai)**. I design and ship **enterprise AI**, **data infrastructure**, and **sovereign AI systems** for government, banking, telecom, and regulated industries—especially across **Africa and the Middle East**.

The problems I care about are practical: **LLM deployment** on infrastructure you control, **retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)** with real tenant isolation, **AI evaluation and governance** you can show auditors, plus **cloud architecture**, **data engineering**, and **Python** services that survive day-2 operations.

## What I build at ADB

Reference platforms and companion code—not slideware. That means air-gapped and sovereign stacks, metered gateways, policy enforcement, observability without SaaS leakage, and acceptance tests in the languages teams actually use (including English, Arabic, and Swahili where the sovereign stack repo specifies them).
